FAQ

Questions

How accurate are the estimates?

They're AI estimates from your description, not lab measurements. For common foods they land close, and more detail (portion, cooking method) makes them better. TeleCal is a tracking aid, not medical advice.

Does it know local dishes?

Yes. Describe anything, like sinigang, a Jollibee Chickenjoy meal or your mom's pasta, and it will ask about portions when it needs to.

What happens to my data?

Your food log is stored on TeleCal's server. To estimate a meal, its description is sent to an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). Nothing else about you is shared.

Why Telegram?

You already have it open. There's nothing to install, no new account, and it works the same on your phone, desktop and the web.

Can I fix a mistake or log yesterday's dinner?

Yes. Edit or delete today's entries, and log to yesterday or any past date with /log yesterday or /log jan 31.

How do I get access?

Tap Request access. It opens TeleCal in Telegram, where you send a request with one tap. You'll get a message in the same chat once you're approved.
